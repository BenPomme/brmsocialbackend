# Cookie policy

Version of 9 September 2026. Complements [privacy](https://www.babyrock.ai/en/privacy/).

## 1. What we use

The public site www.babyrock.ai does not need cookies to show the pages. We store your analytics choice in the browser’s **local storage**, key `br-consent` (value `granted` or `denied`). It does not expire on its own: it lasts until you clear it or tap Cookies in the footer again.

If you **accept**, we turn on Google Analytics 4 (id G-252L7M2L2P). Google may then set, among others:

- `_ga` — tell visitors apart; usually 2 years; Google LLC.
- `_ga_252L7M2L2P` (or the equivalent stream id) — session state; usually 2 years; Google LLC.

Google may process that data in the United States. We do not use advertising cookies. Default consent is **denied**.

The payment app (app.babyrock.ai) uses its own session cookie, needed so the sign-up is not lost. Without it you cannot pay. Basis: necessity for the service you asked for.

## 2. How to accept or refuse

The banner lets you accept or refuse. You can open it again with the Cookies link in the footer. You can also clear cookies and local storage in the browser, or use a blocker.

Refusing does not stop you reading the site or subscribing. It only stops Google Analytics from counting the visit.

## 3. Third parties

Google LLC, United States. Google’s policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Opt out of Analytics: [https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

Stripe, Meta and Google (Business Profile) process data because you use payment, WhatsApp or the manager, not because of a cookie from this banner. That is in [privacy](https://www.babyrock.ai/en/privacy/).
