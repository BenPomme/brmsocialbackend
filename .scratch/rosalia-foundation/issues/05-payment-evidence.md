# 05: Payment evidence is a verified payment, not a Stripe Customer ID

**Category:** bug
**Status:** resolved
**Blocked by:** 01-pay-registration-isolation

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Abandoned checkout (a Stripe Customer ID on a lead) must not be classified or fulfilled as paid.

**Current behavior:**
`isPaidClient` is true whenever `stripeCustomerId` is set. Checkout creates that id before payment. Client status `paye` without a customer id is treated as unpaid. Fulfillment idempotency searches the last 30 billing action JSON rows.

**Desired behavior:**
Sales stage, billing state, and service state stay separate. Payment evidence is a verified successful payment or settled invoice (or explicit trial/manual evidence) tied to this Paid Account and order. Stripe Customer ID is only an external reference. Record product, interval, service period, amount, currency, provider payment id, and test/live mode. Fulfillment is idempotent via a uniqueness constraint on the external payment identity. Concurrent webhook and return-page calls pay once. Test payments do not activate live service. Trial is distinct from paid and has a stored period. Annual records an annual period. Do not infer historical payment from a customer id.

**Key interfaces:**
- Shared paid/trial/active client statuses already used by fulfillment (`paye`, `essai`, `actif`) — do not invent a parallel `paid` client status
- Unique (provider, provider payment id) on payment evidence

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Opened, abandoned and failed checkout remain unpaid
- [ ] A verified successful payment becomes paid once, including concurrent webhook and return-page calls
- [ ] Trial is distinguishable from paid and expires according to its stored period
- [ ] Annual payment records an annual service period; paid conversion from a trial clears conflicting trial state
- [ ] Test payment never activates live service
- [ ] Legacy non-Stripe paid accounts can be represented with explicit verified manual payment evidence

**Out of scope:**
- Turning on Stripe Billing 0.7 % (file 02 ADR)
- Inventing live-mode money movement in this change
